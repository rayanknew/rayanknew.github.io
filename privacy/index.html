Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · App: “ASN” (Meta / Threads API) · Operator: Rayan Knew (@rayanknew), Kyiv, Ukraine

What the app is

ASN is a private publishing tool used only by its owner, Rayan Knew, to prepare, schedule and publish posts on his own Threads account @rayanknew, to read insights of those posts, and to find public posts on topics he writes about. It is not offered to other users and has no sign-up.

Data we access

  • The owner’s own Threads profile, posts, replies and post insights (views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, shares, follower count).
  • Public Threads posts returned by keyword search or public profile lookup, used only to read their text and decide what to write or reply.
  • Replies other people publicly leave under the owner’s posts.

How we use it

Only to write, schedule and publish the owner’s posts and replies and to measure how his posts perform. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, do not build profiles of other users, and do not use data for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on the owner’s own computer in an encrypted-at-rest user account. Public posts read via search are not stored beyond the text needed for a draft and are deleted within 30 days. Insights of the owner’s posts are kept as long as the account exists.

Your rights

If you believe the app holds any data about you (for example the text of your public reply), you can ask for it to be deleted — see Data deletion. We answer within 30 days.

Contact

Direct message on Threads: @rayanknew.
